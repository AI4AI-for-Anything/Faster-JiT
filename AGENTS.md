# Repository instructions

- Keep the upstream workload: JiT-B/16 at 256×256, 128 images per GPU, `blr 5e-5`, 600 epochs with 5 warm-up epochs, BF16 autocast with FP32 master weights, AdamW states and both EMAs (0.9999 and 0.9996). Do not change hyperparameters, data, augmentation, label drop, or the noise and time sampling to gain speed. Default to eight GPUs (the upstream setting) when the GPU count is unspecified.
- The 1.56× (1.5618×) headline is a two-GPU per-step benchmark from a full check (reference → candidate → reference, then numerical gates) on a fixed 32,000-image ImageNet subset. Do not present it as an end-to-end training or time-to-FID speedup, and do not imply that the 3× goal or the 2× fallback was reached.
- Switches are off by default; the measured configuration is `scripts/configs/faster.env` (identical values in `.harness/config.json`). Keep `JIT_COMPILE_EMA=0`: the compiled EMA path failed the revised EMA semantic check. FP8 is not used and was not allowed.
- Numerical status: the final configuration is not bitwise identical to the reference (1,574 of 8,760 recorded tensors). It passed exact-item checks, an isolated EMA check and a 3-seed, 300-step statistical check on the training loss. Full 600-epoch training, FID and online evaluation with the switches have not been run. Do not claim lossless acceleration or fill in quality numbers from the paper.
- The fused QK and SwiGLU kernels are guarded to the measured shapes and print a line containing `fallback` when they fall back to the upstream code. Before trusting a timing, check the log for `fallback`, `skipping cudagraphs due to` and recompile-limit messages, time only steady-state steps after compilation, and alternate reference and candidate runs in independent processes.
- Checkpoints written with `JIT_FUSED_ADAMW=1` keep AdamW step counters on the GPU; resuming them with the switch unset has not been tested.
- The research harness (benchmark, numerical evaluator, experiment ledger) is a separate artifact and is not included. Put new experiments in a new dated directory with source commit, dependency versions and hardware.
- Respect shared-host GPU locks and never stop another user's job. Use public datasets only. Never commit datasets, checkpoints, profiler traces, credentials, private paths or per-file data listings.
